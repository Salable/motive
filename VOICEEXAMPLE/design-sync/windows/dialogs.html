Rules

Only destructive bulk actions ask (Clear). Single-item Remove and Skip act immediately — low cost, visible result.
The Clear button is enabled whenever canClear — pending items OR a stuck awaiting reply with an empty queue. Confirming ALSO resolves that awaiting reply as declined, so Clear can't leave the queue silently stuck.
Escape / outside-click dismisses (on-dismiss). Confirm is the only filled destructive control in the app.
