White active pill on a gray track, top-right of the right pane. Both segments are pressable (nav_queue / nav_settings).
⌘, and the tray's "View Settings" row both dispatch the same nav Msg the tab click does — one command, three entry points.
Built as a real <tabs> control: <button> children lower to segmented triggers with the SDK's house pill treatment (white active pill + hairline, muted inactive label; the track keeps the design's surface_pressed gray via background=). It replaced hand-rolled pressable panels wrapping <text> — static text is drag-selectable in this SDK, so a slightly-moving click on a panel-built tab label started a copy selection instead of the press. A button's label is control chrome and can't be selected.
