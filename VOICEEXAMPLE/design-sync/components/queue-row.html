Rules

Click selects (accent chevron, left edge); click again deselects. Selection drives ↑↓/⌫/⌘↑⌘↓ and follows a reordered item.
Row = tone pillar · number disc · preview (one line, elided, wrap="false") · chevron-up/chevron-down/x ghost icon buttons. Every context item is redundant with a visible control.
Reordering is a model swap — items are safe to move until the moment they dispatch to the sidecar.
Empty state (no pending items): a bordered panel, "Queue is empty. Anything POSTed to /speak lands here." plus an accent-filled "Copy AGENTS.md to get started" button — top-anchored, not vertically centered.
