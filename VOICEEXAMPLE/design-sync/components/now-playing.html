Now speaking card

Occupies the queue view's top slot for as long as an item is current and not awaiting a reply. Disappears entirely when idle (the queue list simply starts at the top) — there is no persistent always-visible strip like the pre-v2 design had.

Rules

Badge: "SPEAKING · #N" or "PAUSED · #N" — one speakBadge() helper picks the word from model.paused.
Body line is the item's one-line preview (wrap="false"), never the full text.
Progress is an ESTIMATE (elapsed ÷ a 190wpm×rate formula, clamped to 97% so it never overshoots before the real "done"), not a measured value — real duration is only known once the item finishes.
When the current item expects_response and finishes speaking, this card's slot is taken over by the Reply card instead (see that component) — the two are mutually exclusive by construction, driven by isSpeakingNow vs. hasAwaitingReply over the same model.current.
