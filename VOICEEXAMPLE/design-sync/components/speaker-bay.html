Speaker bay

The 336px left pane: script wordmark, a wall-mount speaker plate with an inline status LED, and 44px round transport buttons. The whole bay is the window's drag surface (hidden-inset chrome puts the traffic lights over its top-left corner).

The wordmark

<text size="display"><span mono="true">{'TalkBox '}</span></text> — a registered Pacifico script font repointed onto theme.typography.mono_font_id (TalkBox has no other mono text, so the whole app's one mono slot is safe to spend here). The trailing space inside the span gives the script's swash room so the final glyph doesn't clip.

Speaker plate + LED

A single baked icon (app:plate, 224×224: rounded plate, 4 corner screws, inset grille of ~81 dots — one path, under the SDK's 512-element/48-shape icon budget) with a live LED lens overlaid via <stack> at the design's right:20/bottom:12 position. Four mutually-exclusive states, driven by ledPlaying / ledPaused / ledIdle / ledAwaiting:

StateColorMeaning
Playing● greenspeaking now
Paused● amberpaused mid-utterance
Awaiting● accent violet-bluea note finished and is waiting on a reply
Idle● graynothing current
