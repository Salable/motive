TalkBox's actual theme (src/theme.zig), sampled from the running app. Two registers now: the light design below, and a dark register (last section) selected by Settings → Appearance — System follows the macOS appearance live, Light/Dark pin it.

Brand + action

accent #0A7CFF hover #0669D6 active #065CBC
THE primary fill: Play (enabled), active tab pill's text stays ink but the pill itself is surface — accent is reserved for Play, "Copy AGENTS.md to get started", Send, Requeue link text, and the awaiting-reply LED.
destructive #D0342C
Stop-recording button fill only. Skip/Remove/Clear stay plain ink-on-ghost — no destructive fill in the main queue flow.

disabled #E9E9ED
A queue pillar's "gray" tone; the history chip for skipped/failed states.
warning #E8B90A
The plate LED while paused.

Dark register

The same design at night: cards one step LIGHTER than the ground (elevation by lightness), the light ground becomes the ink, the one blue accent brightens a notch (hover LIGHTENS where light's deepens), and each pastel pair inverts into a deep tinted surface with a brightened ink. The paused amber holds — it's a lit lens.
